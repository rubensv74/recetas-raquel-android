# RESEARCH GAPS

1. **Codex PAL 2026 integrated final text** — adoption confirmed; review final integrated annex before implementing reference doses/action levels.
2. **Non-Annex-II ingredient relations** — existence established, but mass ingredient-by-ingredient mapping is not yet reviewed.
3. **WHO/IUIS clinical significance** — nomenclature only; no direct food-level warning from an entry alone.
4. **Cross-reactivity pairs** — each needs a specific source; no family-wide automatic rules.
5. **Processing** — heating, refining, hydrolysis, fermentation etc. are food/process specific.
6. **EU exemptions dataset** — encode from current consolidated Annex II and applicable amendments with validation.
7. **Sulphite concentration** — legal threshold needs concentration context; a generic ingredient name may be insufficient.
8. **Commercial products** — manufacturer evidence is product + label-date specific.
9. **Compound ingredients** — complete, partial and unknown composition require distinct states.
10. **Legacy production data** — repository does not contain the user's production DB, so actual name distribution is not yet quantified.
11. **600 ingredients / 1,200 aliases** — target remains, but no filler entries will be invented.
12. **Other medically indicated restrictions** — architecture supports extension, but no indiscriminate disease-specific logic in v1.
