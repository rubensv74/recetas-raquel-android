# CROSS CONTACT AND PAL

## Distinction

- Direct presence: ingredient/component intentionally present.
- Cross-contact: unintended presence caused by handling, equipment, transport, storage or processing environment.
- PAL (`may contain`): precautionary statement; it is not equivalent to `contains`.

Recommended relation type for label evidence:

```text
DECLARED_MAY_CONTAIN
```

must remain separate from `CONTAINS`.

## EU evidence

Regulation (EU) 2021/382 introduced hygiene requirements around equipment/containers that have been used for Annex II substances before use for foods that do not contain them. This supports prudent cross-contact recommendations but does not let the app certify absence of cross-contact.

## Codex 2026

CAC49 adopted PAL guidelines on 2026-07-06 as an annex to CXS 1-1985. The approach is risk-based and uses reference doses/action levels after mitigation measures.

Project decision for v1: register the source, but do not implement a quantitative PAL engine until the final integrated text and EU/Spanish applicability have been reviewed and explicitly approved.

Never display `No cross-contact risk` or equivalent guarantees.
