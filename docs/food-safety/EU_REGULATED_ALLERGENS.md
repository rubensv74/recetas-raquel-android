# EU REGULATED ALLERGENS

The 14 groups come from Annex II of Regulation (EU) 1169/2011. This is a regulatory list, not a complete list of all possible food allergies.

1. Cereals containing gluten: wheat (including spelt and khorasan wheat), rye, barley, oats, and hybridised strains. The list is exhaustive. Do not reduce to a generic `gluten` flag.
2. Crustaceans and products thereof.
3. Eggs and products thereof. Commission guidance clarifies eggs of all farmed birds.
4. Fish and products thereof, with defined legal exemptions such as certain fish gelatine uses.
5. Peanuts and products thereof.
6. Soybeans and products thereof, with defined exemptions including certain fully refined derivatives.
7. Milk and products thereof, including lactose. Commission guidance clarifies milk from the mammary gland of farmed animals. Milk allergy and lactose intolerance remain distinct conditions.
8. Nuts: almond, hazelnut, walnut, cashew, pecan, Brazil nut, pistachio, macadamia/Queensland nut. The list is exhaustive; preserve species.
9. Celery and products thereof.
10. Mustard and products thereof. Regulation 2024/2512 adds a narrow exemption for behenic acid from mustard seeds under specified purity/process conditions for E470a, E471 and E477, applicable from 2025-04-01.
11. Sesame seeds and products thereof.
12. Sulphur dioxide and sulphites above 10 mg/kg or 10 mg/litre, expressed as total SO2 in ready-to-consume/reconstituted food. Concentration context matters.
13. Lupin and products thereof.
14. Molluscs and products thereof.

## Derivatives and compound ingredients

Annex II covers products derived from listed substances unless a specific exemption applies. The Commission notice also clarifies that compound ingredients still require declaration of Annex II substances inside them.

Exemptions must therefore be first-class, source-backed data, not hidden string rules.
